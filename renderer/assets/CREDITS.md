NOTICE files ship inside the package and must stay with any copy of the app that is passed on.

## Themes

Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Everforest and One are open community colour palettes; the light variants are their published light palettes (Catppuccin Latte, Gruvbox light, Everforest light, One Light).

## Icons

The storm tab's speech bubble is "message-circle" from Lucide, and the settings, choose-image and remove-image icons are based on its "bolt", "image-plus" and "trash-2"; the Appearance, Intelligence and search icons are based on its "palette", "atom" and "search", the Reconnect and Disconnect icons on its "refresh-cw" and "log-out", and the Reset icon on its "rotate-ccw" (https://lucide.dev), ISC License.
